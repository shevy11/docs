Buying a SimSIM plan takes a few taps — choose your destination, pick a plan that suits your trip length and data needs, and pay upfront. There's nothing to configure until you're ready to travel.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the SimSIM app">
    Download and open the SimSIM app, available on iOS and Android.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for your destination">
    Browse by country or region. Regional plans cover multiple countries in a single purchase, so you don't need to buy separately for each stop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Plans vary by data allowance and validity period — from a weekend to a month. Pick the combination that matches your trip length and expected usage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and pay">
    You see the full price before confirming your order. There are no hidden fees and no overage charges — your cost is fixed at checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your email">
    Your eSIM QR code is sent to your registered email address as soon as payment is confirmed. Keep it handy for activation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  All prices are shown in NIS (₪) and include applicable taxes. What you see is what you pay.
</Info>

## Before you buy

<Tip>
  Check that your phone is eSIM-compatible and network-unlocked before purchasing. Once you scan the QR code, the service is activated and refunds are not available.
</Tip>

## Using SimSIM Coins

If you have SimSIM Coins in your account, you can apply them at checkout to reduce the total price — a minimum of 15 coins is required to redeem. Coins are earned automatically on every purchase, so your balance grows with each plan you buy.
