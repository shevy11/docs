If your trip spans multiple countries, a regional plan lets you buy once and stay connected across borders without switching plans. SimSIM regional plans cover popular travel zones in a single purchase.

## What a regional plan covers

A regional plan works in all countries within the designated zone. For example, a Europe plan keeps you connected whether you're in Paris, Rome, or Athens — all on one plan.

## Choosing a regional plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the SimSIM app">
    Launch SimSIM on your device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for your region">
    Search for the region (e.g., "Europe", "Asia", "Americas").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your plan">
    Choose a data allowance and validity period that fits your trip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Purchase and install">
    Purchase and install as you would any SimSIM plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Available regions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Europe">
    Covers EU member states and major European destinations including the UK, Norway, and Switzerland. Ideal for Schengen-zone trips.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Asia">
    Covers Japan, South Korea, Thailand, Vietnam, Singapore, Taiwan, Hong Kong, and more. Check the app for the full country list.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Americas">
    Covers the United States, Canada, Mexico, and select Central and South American countries.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Middle East & Africa">
    Covers the UAE, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, Kenya, and other participating countries in the region.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Global">
    Some plans offer near-global coverage. Check the app for the most current global plan availability.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Regional vs country plans

|  | Country Plan | Regional Plan |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | Single-destination trips | Multi-country itineraries |
| Flexibility | Exact coverage for one country | Works across the whole zone |
| Price | Lower upfront cost | Single purchase for all countries |
| Data sharing | All data for that country | Shared across countries in the zone |

<Tip>
  Regional plans are great value if you're visiting 3 or more countries. For a single-country trip, a country-specific plan usually offers better value.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Always check the specific country list for each regional plan in the app before purchasing, as coverage zones are updated regularly.
</Note>
