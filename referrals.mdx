SimSIM's referral program lets you earn bonus SimSIM Coins by sharing your unique referral code with friends. When a new user signs up and makes a qualifying purchase using your code, you get rewarded.

## How to find your referral code

1. Open the SimSIM app.
2. Go to your **Profile** or **Account** settings.
3. Find the **Refer a Friend** or **Referral** section.
4. Copy your unique referral code or share it directly via the app.

## How it works

1. Share your referral code with friends who haven't used SimSIM before.
2. They sign up using your code.
3. They make their first qualifying purchase.
4. You receive bonus SimSIM Coins in your account.

<Tip>
  Your referral code is personal. Share it with travelers, friends, and family who are planning a trip — the bonus activates only on new accounts.
</Tip>

## Referral terms

<Note>
  Referral bonuses are granted for new user introductions only. Referral history is associated with your account and is cleared if you delete your account.
</Note>

## Using your referral coins

Referral bonus coins are added to your SimSIM Coins balance and work exactly like regular cashback coins — redeem them at checkout (minimum 15 coins) toward any plan or top-up.
