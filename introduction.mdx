SimSIM is a travel eSIM app that gets you mobile data in 200+ countries — instantly, from your phone, in about a minute. An eSIM is a digital SIM card built into your phone: there's no plastic card to swap, no SIM tray to fumble with at the airport. SimSIM runs as a second eSIM alongside your regular SIM, so your phone number stays exactly where it is and calls and texts keep arriving as normal while SimSIM handles the data abroad.

## What SimSIM is not

<Note>
  SimSIM is a data-only eSIM. Voice calls and SMS are not included. Apps like WhatsApp, Telegram, Google Maps, and Uber work perfectly over data.
</Note>

## Supported apps

SimSIM gives you a data connection that works with all the apps you already rely on when you travel. WhatsApp, Telegram, Google Maps, Airbnb, Booking.com, Uber, and Trip.com all work seamlessly over your SimSIM data connection — no extra setup required.
