SimSIM's cancellation policy follows Israeli Consumer Protection Law. You have a 14-day window to cancel after purchase — but only if you haven't yet activated your eSIM by scanning the QR code.

<Warning>
  Once you scan your eSIM QR code, the service is considered fully delivered. Refunds are not possible after activation. Verify device compatibility and plan details before purchasing.
</Warning>

## Cancellation Conditions

| Situation | Cancellation possible? |
|---|---|
| Within 14 days, QR code **NOT** scanned | ✅ Yes — cancellation fee applies |
| Within 14 days, QR code scanned/activated | ❌ No — service delivered |
| After 14 days | ❌ No |
| Incompatible or locked device | ❌ No |
| Local network issues in your destination | ❌ No — reviewed case by case |

## Cancellation Fee

<Info>
  If you cancel within the 14-day window before activation, a cancellation fee of **5% of the total order value** or **₪100** (whichever is lower) applies. The remaining amount is refunded to your original payment method.
</Info>

## How to Cancel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Email support">
    Email [support@simsimglobal.com](mailto:support@simsimglobal.com) with your order number and cancellation request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include your details">
    Include your name and the email address on the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do not scan your QR code">
    Do **NOT** scan your QR code while the cancellation is being processed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Await confirmation">
    We'll confirm your cancellation and process the refund within the legal timeframe.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Exceptions

<Note>
  Technical issues with local networks or infrastructure are not grounds for a full refund, as SimSIM cannot control third-party network availability. In exceptional circumstances, cases are reviewed at management's discretion. Contact support immediately if you experience a technical issue.
</Note>

## SimSIM Coins on Cancelled Orders

If you earned SimSIM Coins on a cancelled order, those coins are deducted from your balance. If you used coins to pay for the order, they are refunded to your account.
