If you run low on data mid-trip, you can top up directly inside the SimSIM app in seconds. There's no need to buy a new eSIM, reinstall a profile, or scan a new QR code — your existing connection stays active throughout.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the SimSIM app">
    Launch SimSIM on your phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to your active plan">
    Your active plan is displayed on the home screen. Tap it to see your remaining data balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Top Up">
    Select **Top Up** or **Add more data** on your plan screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an amount and confirm payment">
    Pick the additional data amount that suits your needs and confirm payment. The full cost is shown before you commit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your balance updates immediately">
    Your data balance is refreshed straight away — no waiting, no restart required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Low-data alerts

<Info>
  SimSIM sends you a notification when your data is running low, so you have time to top up before going offline.
</Info>

## Using SimSIM Coins for a top-up

You can use SimSIM Coins toward the cost of a top-up just like a regular plan purchase. A minimum of 15 coins is required to redeem at checkout, and any coins you earn from the top-up are added to your balance automatically.

## Buying a new plan instead

<Note>
  If your current plan has expired (not just run out of data), you'll need to purchase a new plan from the app rather than topping up. Contact support at support@simsimglobal.com if you're unsure whether your plan is expired or just empty.
</Note>
