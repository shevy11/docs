## How to check a specific country

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the SimSIM app">
    Launch SimSIM on your device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for your destination">
    Tap the search bar and type your destination country.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse available plans">
    If plans are available, they'll appear with pricing and data options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="No plans shown?">
    If no plans are shown, that country may not yet be covered — contact [support@simsimglobal.com](mailto:support@simsimglobal.com).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Network quality

SimSIM connects you to trusted local networks in each country. While SimSIM works to provide the best available signal, local network performance can vary by area, especially in rural or remote locations.

<Note>
  Coverage in rural areas and at sea may be limited. For travel to very remote destinations, check availability in the app before your trip.
</Note>
