## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the SimSIM app">
    Grab the SimSIM app from the [App Store](https://apple.com/app-store) or [Google Play](https://play.google.com). It's free, and your account is ready to use straight away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your destination and choose a plan">
    Search for your destination country or browse regional plans. All prices are shown upfront — no hidden fees, no contracts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code to install your eSIM">
    After purchase, SimSIM generates a QR code. Open your phone's eSIM settings, scan the code, and your data plan installs in seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land connected — no kiosk, no swapping">
    Touch down at your destination and your SimSIM plan activates automatically. No physical SIM to swap, no airport queues, no roaming surprises.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  SimSIM is data-only. Voice calls and SMS are not included — but apps like WhatsApp and Telegram work great over data.
</Note>
