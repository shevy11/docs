You need an internet connection to install an eSIM. Install it before your trip while you have reliable Wi-Fi.

## Install from the app

1. Open **My eSIMs**.
2. Open the eSIM with the **Ready to install** status.
3. Tap **Install eSIM** and follow your device's prompts.
4. If you need another method, open the menu and choose **Install on another device** to view the QR or manual details.

## After installation

- Set SimSIM as your mobile data line.
- Enable data roaming for the SimSIM line.
- Keep your regular SIM active for calls and SMS.
- Avoid deleting the eSIM while it is usable. A deleted profile cannot be reinstalled from the app.

If installation fails, confirm that you are online, your phone supports eSIM, and the phone is carrier-unlocked. Then see [Troubleshooting](/en/troubleshooting).
