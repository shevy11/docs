## Before you start

- **Install before you travel.** Downloading the profile requires an internet connection, so install at home while you have reliable Wi-Fi.
- **Confirm your device supports eSIM and is carrier-unlocked.** See [Device compatibility](/en/device-compatibility), or check your model against the [full device list](https://simsimglobal.com/esim-compatible-devices) on our site.
- **Switch your data line only after you land** — not before your flight.

<Note>
**Your eSIM will not be named "SimSIM" on your device.**

It appears under a local network's name at your destination — this is normal. Wherever these guides say **"the eSIM you set up"**, this is the line they mean.
</Note>

## Alternative installation methods

If the standard install button doesn't work for your situation — for example, you're setting up a phone for someone else, or the camera on the target device isn't working — you can install using a QR code or by entering the details manually.

## After installation

- **Keep your primary line active** for calls and SMS, but turn off its mobile data so you don't accumulate roaming charges from your own carrier.
- **Don't delete the eSIM** while the plan is still valid. A deleted profile cannot be reinstalled from the app.
- You can check your remaining data at any time under [Managing data](/en/managing-data) in the app.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does my eSIM show another company's name instead of SimSIM?">
    Because the eSIM connects to a local network at your destination, and your device displays that network's name. The installation is working correctly — this is exactly what should happen.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I installed the eSIM but have no internet at my destination">
    In most cases **data roaming** is switched off on the new line. Turn it on, and also confirm that the eSIM you set up is selected as your mobile data line.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will I still receive calls to my primary number?">
    Yes. As long as your primary line stays active and set as the voice line, calls and SMS will continue to reach you as usual.

    Note that calls and messages abroad are billed by your own carrier, according to your plan's terms. Your SimSIM eSIM provides data only and does not affect those charges.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Should I install before the flight or after landing?">
    Install before your flight while you have stable Wi-Fi, since installation needs an internet connection. Switch your data line only once you arrive.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Installation failed — what now?">
    Confirm you have a stable internet connection, that your device [supports eSIM](https://simsimglobal.com/esim-compatible-devices), and that it is carrier-unlocked. If the problem persists, see [Troubleshooting](/en/troubleshooting) or contact support.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
