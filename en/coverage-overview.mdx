Coverage and plan availability depend on the destination and the plan. The app's catalog is the current source of truth.

## Check a destination

1. Open **Shop**.
2. Search for a country or city.
3. Review the plans returned for that destination.
4. Open a plan to inspect included countries, networks, speed, validity, and data details.

A plan may use more than one local partner network. Signal and speed depend on local infrastructure, congestion, and location.

For trips across multiple countries, compare [regional plans](/en/regional-plans).
