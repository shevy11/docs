Open an eSIM from **My eSIMs** to see its status, remaining data, validity, network, package details, and available actions.

## eSIM statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Ready to install** | The profile is available but has not been installed on a device. |
| **Connected** | The profile is installed and active. |
| **eSIM turned off** | The profile is installed but disabled in device settings. |
| **Data used** | The package has no remaining data. Top-up availability depends on the plan. |
| **Expired** | The package validity ended. Buy a new eSIM to continue. |
| **Suspended** | The network provider paused the profile. Contact support. |
| **Unavailable** | The profile was cancelled or revoked and cannot be used. |

You can rename an eSIM, copy its ICCID, open package details, contact support, and archive terminal eSIMs when those actions are available.

<Warning>
  Do not delete an active or suspended eSIM from your phone. Reinstalling a deleted profile may not be possible.
</Warning>
