SimSIM gives you mobile data through a digital eSIM. Choose a plan for your destination, install the eSIM on your phone, and use it as your travel data line.

## What you can do

- Browse country and regional plans.
- Check whether your device supports eSIM.
- Install a purchased eSIM from the app.
- Monitor data and validity.
- Add data when your plan supports top-ups.
- Get help with account, payment, and connection issues.

SimSIM is data-only. Keep your regular SIM active for your phone number, calls, and SMS, and use SimSIM for mobile data.
