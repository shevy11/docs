## How it works

1. Open **Account → Wallet & referrals**.
2. Copy your referral link or code and share it.
3. Your friend signs up and enters the code at checkout.
4. They activate a qualifying first plan over \$5.
5. Your \$2 SimSIM Coins reward becomes available after 14 days.

The app shows pending and rewarded referrals in your wallet activity. A referral reward may remain pending until the qualifying plan is activated and the eligibility period ends.

Your friend receives the referral benefit shown at checkout. Referral eligibility and program rules are governed by the official [Terms of Service](https://simsimglobal.com/terms-of-service).
