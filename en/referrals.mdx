## How it works

1. Open **Account → Wallet & referrals**.
2. Copy your referral link or code and share it.
3. Your friend signs up and enters the code at checkout.
4. They activate any plan — no minimum purchase required.
5. Your \$1 credit reward becomes available 7 days after their plan activates.

The app shows pending and rewarded referrals in your wallet activity. A referral reward may remain pending until the qualifying plan is activated and the eligibility period ends.

Your friend receives 15% off their first plan (no minimum). Your $1 credit is issued 7 days after their plan activates. Referral eligibility and program rules are governed by the official [Terms of Service](https://simsimglobal.com/terms-of-service).
