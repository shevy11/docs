---
title: "Change language, currency, and appearance"
sidebarTitle: "Preferences"
description: "Customize the SimSIM app from **Account**."
---

Open **Account** and choose the setting you want to change.

## Language

Choose **English**, **Hebrew**, or **Default**. Default follows the device language when it is supported; otherwise the app uses English. The app reloads after a language change so the new direction and translations apply everywhere.

## Currency

Choose **USD**, **EUR**, **ILS**, or **Default**. Default uses the device currency when supported and otherwise falls back to USD. A currency choice changes price display; it does not change the plan or its data allowance.

## Appearance

Choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **Default** to control the app theme.
