Buying a plan takes a few taps:

1. Open **Shop**.
2. Search for your country, city, or region.
3. Open a plan and review its data, validity, included destinations, networks, and price.
4. Continue to checkout and confirm that your device is compatible and carrier-unlocked.
5. Review the total and complete payment.

After payment, the order appears in **My eSIMs**. Open it there to install the eSIM and view its details.

<Tip>
  Check device compatibility and the plan's coverage before you pay. The plan's own details are the source of truth for included countries and validity.
</Tip>
