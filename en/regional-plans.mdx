A regional plan covers multiple countries in one purchase. The countries included depend on the specific plan.

## Choose a regional plan

1. Open **Shop**.
2. Search for a region.
3. Open a plan and review its included-country list.
4. Confirm that every destination on your itinerary is included.
5. Buy and install the plan as usual.

Do not rely only on the region name. Always check the plan's current country list before payment because coverage can change between plans.

Regional plans share the plan's data allowance across the included destinations. They do not create a separate allowance for each country.
