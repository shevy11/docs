---
title: "Manage saved cards"
sidebarTitle: "Saved Cards"
description: "Add, select, and remove saved payment cards from your account."
---

If you are signed in, open **Account → Saved Cards**.

## Add a card

Tap **Add a card** and complete the secure card form. The card appears in your saved-card list after it is saved.

## Choose a primary card

Tap a saved card to make it the primary card for future checkout. You can still choose another available card during a purchase.

## Remove a card

Tap the remove action beside a card and confirm. You cannot remove the primary card without choosing another card first.

Saved cards are optional. You can also add a new card during checkout.
