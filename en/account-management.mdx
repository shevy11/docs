The **Account** tab contains your account controls and links to related features.

## Guest access

You can browse plans as a guest. Sign in to sync your eSIMs and account data across devices.

## Account features

- **Wallet & referrals:** view SimSIM Coins and invite friends.
- **Language:** choose English, Hebrew, or the device default.
- **Currency:** choose USD, EUR, ILS, or the device default.
- **Appearance:** choose light, dark, or the device default.
- **Saved Cards:** add, select, or remove saved payment cards.
- **Help center:** open the latest support documentation.

## Sign out or delete your account

Use the actions at the bottom of **Account**. Account deletion is permanent. Review the official [Privacy Policy](https://simsimglobal.com/privacy-policy) before deleting your account.
