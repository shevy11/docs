Your phone needs both eSIM support and an unlocked status to use SimSIM.

## Check in the app

1. Open **Shop**.
2. Tap the device compatibility check.
3. Follow the instructions for your phone.
4. If the device is supported, continue to choose a plan.

## Check manually

- On iPhone, look in **Settings → General → About** for an eSIM option and check **Network Provider Lock**.
- On Android, search **Settings** for **eSIM** or **Add eSIM**. Menu names vary by manufacturer.

A carrier-locked phone may reject a SimSIM profile even when the model supports eSIM. Contact your mobile provider if the device shows a lock or restriction.

<Warning>
  Check compatibility before purchasing and installing. If you are unsure, use the in-app check or contact support before you scan the installation details.
</Warning>
