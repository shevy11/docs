This page summarizes the key points from SimSIM's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. SimSIM is operated by a private business registered in Israel. Governing law is Israeli law; exclusive jurisdiction is the courts of Tel Aviv-Jaffa.

<Note>
  The full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service are available in Hebrew at [simsimglobal.com](https://www.simsimglobal.com). The summaries below are provided for convenience.
</Note>

## Data We Collect

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Information you provide">
    Name, email, phone number, country, plan details, and billing information. Credit card data is **NOT** stored by SimSIM — it is handled by PCI-DSS compliant payment processors.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Automatically collected data">
    IP address, device type, operating system, browser, session data, and cookie identifiers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="eSIM usage data">
    Your eSIM number, destination country, activation date, expiry date, data usage, and real-time network status.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SimSIM Coins data">
    Your coins balance, transaction history, total spend, last purchase date, and referral conversion data.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How We Use Your Data

We use your data to deliver the eSIM service, process orders, provide customer support, issue invoices, improve the app experience, prevent fraud, manage SimSIM Coins, and send marketing communications (with your consent).

## Your Rights

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access, correct, or delete your data">
    You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete your account in the app">
    To delete your account and associated data, go to **Profile → Account → Delete Account** in the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit a data request">
    For data access or correction requests, email [support@simsimglobal.com](mailto:support@simsimglobal.com).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Data Retention

<Note>
  Purchase records and invoice data are retained anonymously for up to 7 years per Israeli tax law, even after account deletion. Active eSIMs remain usable until their original expiry date.
</Note>

## Children's Privacy

<Note>
  SimSIM is not intended for users under 16. If we discover data collected from a minor, it will be deleted immediately.
</Note>

## Service Eligibility

You must be 18 or older and legally competent to purchase from SimSIM. The service is for personal use only — commercial resale or distribution is prohibited without a written agreement.

## Contact

For privacy or legal inquiries, contact [support@simsimglobal.com](mailto:support@simsimglobal.com).
