Your SimSIM account gives you access to your active plans, purchase history, SimSIM Coins balance, and settings — all from the app.

## Creating an account

1. Download the SimSIM app (iOS or Android).
2. Open the app and tap **Sign Up**.
3. Enter your email address and create a password — or sign in with Google or Apple.
4. Verify your email address.
5. You're ready to browse plans and make your first purchase.

## Deleting your account

<Warning>
  Deleting your account is permanent. Your SimSIM Coins balance and referral history will be erased. Active eSIMs remain usable until their original expiry date.
</Warning>

1. Open the SimSIM app.
2. Go to **Profile → Account**.
3. Tap **Delete Account** and confirm.
4. If you signed in with Google or Apple, you don't need a separate password to confirm deletion.

<Note>
  Purchase records and invoice data are retained anonymously for up to 7 years as required by Israeli tax law, even after account deletion.
</Note>
